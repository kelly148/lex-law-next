ENGAGEMENT LETTER

Dear Client,

We are pleased to confirm our engagement to represent you in connection with [Matter Description]. This letter sets forth the terms and conditions of our representation.

1. SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION
Our representation is limited to the matter described above. We will not represent you in any other matter unless we enter into a separate written engagement agreement.

2. FEES AND BILLING
Our fees for this matter will be billed at our standard hourly rates, currently ranging from $350 to $650 per hour depending on the attorney assigned. Flat-fee arrangements, where offered, will be set forth in a separate written addendum to this letter. All fee arrangements are effective from the date of this letter.

A retainer of $5,000 is due upon execution of this letter. This retainer is non-refundable and will be applied against fees and costs as they are incurred. Any unused portion of the retainer will be applied to the final invoice.

3. COSTS AND EXPENSES
In addition to our fees, you will be responsible for all costs and expenses incurred in connection with your matter, including filing fees, court reporter fees, expert witness fees, and travel expenses.

4. INDEMNIFICATION
You agree to indemnify and hold harmless the firm and its attorneys from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or resulting from your own acts, omissions, or negligence, including any third-party claims asserted against the firm that arise from your conduct or instructions.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
All communications between you and the firm, whether in person, by telephone, by email, by text message, or through any cloud-based or digital platform, are protected by the attorney-client privilege to the fullest extent permitted by law. You agree to use reasonable care to maintain the confidentiality of all communications and documents shared with the firm.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any dispute arising out of or relating to this engagement letter or our representation shall be governed by the laws of the State of [State] and shall be resolved by binding arbitration in [City, State] under the rules of the American Arbitration Association. The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this engagement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Upon termination, you will be responsible for all fees and costs incurred through the date of termination.

Please sign and return a copy of this letter to confirm your agreement to these terms.

Sincerely,

[Attorney Name]
