This engagement letter draft is generally competent but requires several revisions before it is ready for client delivery.

First, the confidentiality provisions in Section 5 do not adequately address digital communications, including email and cloud storage. Given the sensitive nature of the matters handled by the firm, this gap creates meaningful risk.

Second, the dispute resolution clause should specify the governing jurisdiction and whether arbitration or litigation is the preferred mechanism. The current language is silent on both points.

Third, the retainer amount referenced in Section 2 should include language clarifying whether it is refundable or non-refundable, and under what circumstances any unused portion will be returned.

These are substantive issues that should be addressed before the letter is executed.
